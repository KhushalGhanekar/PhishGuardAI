# PhishGuardAI

AI-Powered Cybersecurity Platform for Phishing Detection & Digital Asset Protection

PhishGuard AI is an intelligent cybersecurity web application designed to help users identify phishing attacks, suspicious URLs, and online threats in real time. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence and cybersecurity best practices, the platform aims to make digital safety more accessible, understandable, and proactive for everyone.

🌐 Live Demo:
https://khushalghanekar.github.io/PhishGuardAI/

🚀 Project Overview:

Cybercriminals continuously use phishing attacks and fraudulent websites to steal sensitive information such as passwords, banking credentials, and personal data. Many users struggle to identify these threats before becoming victims.

PhishGuard AI was developed as a prototype solution to address this growing challenge. The platform provides intelligent analysis and cybersecurity guidance, helping users make safer decisions while navigating the internet.

🏆 Solution Challenge 2026 India – Build with AI:

PhishGuard AI was developed as a team project for the Solution Challenge 2026 India – Build with AI, organized through the Google Developer ecosystem.

The project was built under the:

🔐 Digital Asset Protection – Open Innovation Track:

This track encouraged participants to design innovative solutions that protect users' digital assets and address real-world cybersecurity challenges.
Our team chose to focus on the increasing threat of phishing attacks, malicious websites, and online scams that affect millions of internet users every year.
Although our project was not selected among the Top 100 submissions, the experience was incredibly valuable and helped us gain practical knowledge in AI integration, cybersecurity, product development, and teamwork.

🎯 Problem Statement:

Phishing remains one of the most common and dangerous cyber threats today.
These attacks can lead to:

- Identity theft
- Financial fraud
- Credential compromise
- Data breaches
- Loss of digital assets
- Reduced trust in online platforms

Many users cannot easily differentiate between legitimate and malicious websites.

PhishGuard AI aims to bridge this gap by providing intelligent threat analysis and cybersecurity awareness tools.

✨ Key Features
🔍 URL Threat Analysis
Analyze suspicious URLs and identify phishing indicators.
🤖 AI-Powered Insights
Leverages AI to provide intelligent security assessments and recommendations.
🛡️ Phishing Detection
Detects patterns commonly associated with phishing attempts and malicious websites.
⚡ Real-Time Feedback
Provides instant analysis and guidance to users.
📚 Cybersecurity Awareness
Helps users understand online threats and adopt safer browsing practices.
📱 Responsive User Interface
Modern and responsive design optimized for desktop and mobile devices.
🧠 AI Integration
Gemini API

PhishGuard AI uses the Google Gemini API to deliver intelligent analysis, threat detection assistance, and cybersecurity recommendations.
This enables users to receive AI-powered insights when evaluating suspicious links and potential online threats.
